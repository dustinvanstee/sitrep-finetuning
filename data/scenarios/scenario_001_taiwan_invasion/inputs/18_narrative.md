
=== INTELLIGENCE NARRATIVE SUMMARY ===
Over the reporting window, multiple high‑confidence sources corroborate a coordinated PLA amphibious campaign centered on the Senkaku Islands. A flash alert on 20 January (200915Z) documented the initiation of a full‑scale assault, with satellite imagery and intercepted communications confirming an overwhelming fire‑supremacy posture and the fragmentation of Taiwanese coastal defenses. Subsequent imagery on 28 September (2025‑06:15Z) and 3 November (202511031430Z) captured concentrated amphibious task forces assembling east of the island chain, staging landing craft, and positioning anti‑ship missile batteries near Ishigaki, indicating a preparatory phase for a large‑scale landing on Dengbu Island and adjacent objectives. Concurrently, a missile warning on 25 September (2025‑14:32Z) identified IRBM launches from submarines near the Senkaku area, projecting a northeast trajectory toward the Taiwan Strait and underscoring an integrated air‑to‑ground strike package.

Analytically, the pattern suggests a shift from limited probing to a decisive amphibious thrust aimed at establishing a mainland foothold on Taiwan’s eastern coast, leveraging maritime superiority, electronic warfare, and precision missile strikes to degrade Taiwanese resistance. The clear‑sky weather conditions and high visibility reported across the observations facilitate operational execution and increase the likelihood of successful landings, while the involvement of multiple platforms (submarines, surface vessels, fixed‑wing aircraft, and helicopters) reflects a multilayered operational design. However, key uncertainties remain regarding the operational tempo of the assault, the readiness and efficacy of Taiwanese counter‑measures, potential diplomatic or allied responses, and the logistics required to sustain a prolonged occupation. Additional intelligence on Taiwanese force posture, civilian impact, and possible external support will be critical to refine the projection of PLA capabilities and the broader strategic implications of this escalation.


=== OSINT / CIVIL SIGNALS ===
OSINT
- PLA Navy AIS tracks show three Type 901 amphibious assault ships and two landing craft moving eastward toward the Senkaku Islands, consistent with assault group staging.
- Taiwanese civil aviation radar logs indicate multiple unmanned surveillance drones and low‑altitude maritime patrol aircraft operating over the East China Sea near the islands.
- Local fishermen on social media report increased sonar activity and the presence of Chinese naval vessels conducting live‑fire drills.
- Commercial shipping AIS data shows a 30% reduction in vessels transiting the Miyako Strait, with many vessels rerouting southward.
Assessment: Indicators suggest moderate escalation with preparatory amphibious movements, but no confirmed launch orders.
